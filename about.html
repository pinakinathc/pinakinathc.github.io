Professional Information: 
I currently work in the Future Technology Group at Sony Interactive Entertainment (Sony PlayStation), where I design real-time algorithms for AI-generated content in games. My focus areas include frame interpolation, super-resolution, and 3D generation.

I completed my PhD at the SketchX Lab, part of the Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP) at the University of Surrey, United Kingdom. I was supervised by Yi-Zhe Song and Tao Xiang. My thesis was examined by Philip Isola (MIT) and Josef Kittler (CVSSP, University of Surrey). One of the chapters from my PhD was recognized as a Best Paper Award Candidate at CVPR 2023 (Top-12 papers). Currently, I am a Visiting Researcher at SketchX.
	
In the summer of 2021, I interned at Adobe Research (London), working with Tuanfeng Wang and Duygu Ceylan. Our collaboration resulted in an oral presentation at 3DV 2022 (Top-13 papers).
	
Before my PhD, I spent nearly two years (2018–2020) as a full-time research assistant at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata. I was supervised by Umapada Pal. In January 2022, I was invited back as a Visiting Scientist.
	
I actively review for top-tier conferences and journals, including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, TPAMI, AISTATS, NeurIPS, ICLR, Transactions on Multimedia, Computer & Graphics, Pattern Recognition, PRL, ICPR, and Machine Intelligence Research.
